I'm Lily, a second-year Ph.D. student in mathematics at the University of Pittsburgh working primarily in applied mathematics, mathematical biology, and dynamical systems. My current research with Dr. Sabrina Streipert focuses on state-dependent delay differential equations. I am particularly interested in mathematical modeling of carnivorous plants, plant ecology, benthic ecology, and eutrophication, as well as the study and construction of spaces of finite elements. My broader interests include differential geometry, scientific computing, optimization, machine learning, and computational biology and chemistry.

Outside of mathematics, I'm interested in public policy, computer networks, urban geography, simulation and procedural generation, hardware, music production, speed-typing, and competitive rhythm games. I'm always happy to hear from people interested in collaborating.
